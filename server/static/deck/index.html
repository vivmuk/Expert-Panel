02 The problem
Consultants hand over a deck. Teams needed a working agent.
A

The advice stops where the work starts.

A strategy deck says "use AI in sales ops." It doesn't say which step, which owner, or what the agent is allowed to do.

B

Pilots live on a new platform. Teams don't.

People work in Slack, email and the CRM. An agent that needs its own tab gets opened twice and forgotten.

C

Nothing is left behind to maintain.

When the engagement ends there are no instructions, playbooks or guardrails that the next person or agent can pick up.

03 What does

Three steps from a problem in chat to agents running in your tools.

→ 01

Panel thinking

A custom panel of AI expert personas, staffed for this problem, argues it out using live web and X research with citations.

  • Now / Next / Later recommendations
  • Red Team attack on the plan
→ 02

Workflow blueprint

Work Chart maps the process as it runs today and as it runs with agents, one step at a time. Every step gets an owner.

A harness generator turns the blueprint into a downloadable agent kit, installed where the team already works.

  • CLAUDE.md, playbooks, agents, skills
  • Model routing and state schema
  • Anti-patterns and an improvement loop
Also

The classic consulting work, blueprinting and strategy, comes out of the same panels. The main focus is building harnesses and agents for client teams.

12 Business model Aspirational · plans proposed, untested

Fixed-price sprint to build. Retainer to run.

Entry

Connect your agent. First engagement free.

A Deep Dive or Quick Pulse on the client's real problem, delivered in chat, before any contract. Expert-Panel estimates the model cost before it runs, so the giveaway has a known ceiling.

Proposed
Agent Sprint · per workflow
Fixed scope per workflow · proposed

One workflow taken from problem statement to installed agents. Fixed scope so a team lead can approve it without a procurement cycle.

  • Panel report, Red Team and Work Chart
  • Agent kit generated and tailored
  • Installed in the team's tools
  • Two weeks, one workflow
Agent Ops retainer
Monthly plan · proposed

Agents drift as tools, models and the work change. The retainer keeps the kit current and pays for the next workflow's scoping.

  • Monitor runs and model costs
  • Update skills, playbooks and routing
  • Quarterly Red Team on live agents
  • Next workflow scoped in the same chat
What we'd test in the program
  • Who signs: the team lead who owns the workflow, or IT and procurement.
  • Whether one fixed price holds for regulated teams such as banking and insurance.
  • Model cost per installed agent per month, from Expert-Panel's per-call cost ledger.
